Business Requirements Document (BRD)
Employee Leave Management System

Business Objective

To reduce approval waiting time and the number of communication channels an employee has to go through when requesting leave, by unifying the request, tracking, and approval process in one place, and ensuring approval continuity even when the direct manager is unavailable through a pre assigned Delegate Approver.

Scope

A medium sized company. The project covers: submitting a leave request, approving it, and calculating leave balance for three leave types (annual, sick, emergency).

Stakeholders

Employee
Direct Manager (Approver)
Delegate Approver
HR

Business Requirements

The company needs HR to record each employee's leave balance, with the system automatically indicating when an employee's balance is depleted.
The company needs every leave request to automatically route through the direct manager (or their delegate when unavailable) and then HR, with the request status clearly visible to the employee at all times.
